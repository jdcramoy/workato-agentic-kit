# Builder Enablement

You want to build something with AI. This is the place that gets you from idea to a running app without reading the full governance policy.

The rules live in the [Vibe-Coded App Governance Policy](https://workato.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/extbt/pages/2553184311/Vibe-Coded+App+Governance+Policy). You do not need to read all of it. These pages give you the parts you need, in the order you need them.

## Start here

1. [Tier and SDLC quick reference](#) - one printable card. What tier is your app, and what does that tier ask of you at each stage.
2. [Platform decision tree](#) - two questions (what data, who do you share with) and a flowchart that lands you on a tier and a place to build.
3. [FAQ](#) - the questions builders actually ask, including "I already built something, am I in trouble?"

If you read nothing else: the tier comes from the data your app touches and the people you share it with, and sharing wider only ever moves you up, never down.
